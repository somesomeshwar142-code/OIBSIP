Biography & Lifetime Contributions

From humble beginnings in Rameswaram to the highest constitutional office of India.

1. Early Life & Indomitable Spirit

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Growing up in a modest family, his father operated a boat while his mother managed their home. To support his family and pay for his education, young Kalam distributed newspapers after school. His relentless curiosity and love for mathematics drove him to pursue Aeronautical Engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT).

2. The Missile Man of India

Joining the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) in 1969, Dr. Kalam served as the Project Director for India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III), successfully deploying the Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980. Moving to the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), he spearheaded the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP), leading the successful development of self-reliant defense capabilities including the Agni and Prithvi missiles.

3. The People's President

In 2002, Dr. Kalam was elected as the 11th President of India with overwhelming cross-party support. Affectionately called "The People's President," he transformed Rashtrapati Bhavan into an open, welcoming center for students, scientists, and citizens. He was celebrated for his humility, accessible nature, and his refusal to accept lavish privileges.

4. Teaching, Writing & Eternal Legacy

Following his presidency, Dr. Kalam devoted his remaining years to his truest passion: teaching. He authored inspiring bestsellers such as Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020. On July 27, 2015, while delivering a lecture to students at IIM Shillong, he collapsed doing what he loved most. His life remains an enduring symbol of integrity, patriotism, and scientific excellence.
